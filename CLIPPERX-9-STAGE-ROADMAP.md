# ClipperX — Nine-stage path to a director-grade system

“Absolute best” cannot be guaranteed for every unseen video, but nine focused stages are a realistic path to a professional, highly reliable system without treating one enormous prompt as a solution.

1. **Story Intelligence — complete.** Build factual events, causal arcs, setup/action/outcome/reaction links and simultaneous coverage requirements.
2. **Composition Director — complete.** Select natural shared crops, splits, grids, story bands, action pans and continuity-safe transitions.
3. **Multi-Viewport Renderer — complete.** Execute layouts, stabilize each camera, render captions, measure quality and perform one bounded correction.
4. **Perceptual Grounding — complete in v1.0.0.** Separate camera shake, reconnect identities, find small story objects and construct interaction evidence.
5. **Social and Audio Intelligence — complete in v1.1.0.** Understand overlap, prosody, laughter onset, joke setup/payoff, interruption, agreement and group reaction intensity.
6. **Action and Outcome Specialists — complete in v1.2.0.** Add sports/game/object-specific trajectory, possession, contact, target, score/success/failure and reaction verification.
7. **Editorial Style and Retention Director — complete in v1.3.0.** Control hooks, pacing, intentional pauses, visual rhythm, profiles, transitions and platform-specific variants without harming story clarity.
8. **Multimodal Quality Supervisor and Benchmark Lab — complete in v1.4.0.** Critique the rendered video, run targeted rerenders, compare against deterministic quality gates and prevent regressions across difficult scenarios.
9. **Production Hardening — complete in v2.0.0.** Hardware-aware routing, verified caching and resumability, resource limits, installer validation, privacy controls, crash recovery and release-grade performance profiling are implemented.

After Stage 9, improvement should become benchmark-driven releases rather than adding more large architectural stages.
